Whensdays - build a following, fill the room

Make a page for yourself as a performer, or for your troupe, band, venue, or comedy night. Fans follow it with one link, and every show you post lands in their feed, their inbox, and their calendar.

Whensdays also runs a free group scheduler for recurring friend groups and clubs at whensdays.com/free-scheduler - book clubs, run clubs, game nights, jams - so the same group finds when everyone's free, locks the winning dates as a series, and keeps the streak alive; members vote and RSVP with no app and no account.

How it works

  • Pages: a performer, troupe, band, venue, or comedy night gets a page fans follow with one link - new shows post straight to followers' feed, inbox, and calendar
  • Availability polls: exact times, this week, this month, or a general day-and-time grid
  • Schedule one date or several at once as a recurring series
  • Groups with streaks, re-polls, recaps, and reminders
  • Calendar sync (Google and Apple) and one-tap RSVP straight from email
