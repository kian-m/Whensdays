Do I need an account to use the scheduler?

No. Start a plan, share the link, and everyone can vote and RSVP with just their name. No signup required to respond.

Can it lock more than one date at a time?

Yes. A whole series of dates locks in one tap, not one date at a time, and the group's calendars sync both ways as the plan comes together.
